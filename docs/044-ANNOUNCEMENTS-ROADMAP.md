## ANN-03 dispatch reliability correction — 2026-11-13

The fallback can enqueue already-due jobs together after extended outages; the 3-second delays are for normal operation, not a hard global rate guarantee. A dedicated queue/worker is deliberately not added.

## ANN-03 — Reseller-only Sales Bot notification (operator-approved 2026-11-13)

**Audience and delivery:** Only active users with current `reseller` role, positive Telegram account matching a reachable private Sales Bot contact, are queued.
